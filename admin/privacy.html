Privacy Policy — Afzal E Services

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Afzal E Services ("we", "our") provides job advertisements, scholarship information, and a private teaching-jobs portal for Mardan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. This policy explains what data our Android app collects and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data (teaching portal only): if you create an organization or teacher account, we store your email address, and the profile details you enter (institution name, subjects, qualifications, district). Teacher profiles are private by default.
  • Job content you submit: teaching vacancies posted by organizations (titles, descriptions, deadlines). Vacancies are reviewed by an administrator before appearing publicly.
  • Device notification token: used only to deliver the job alerts and deadline reminders you enable in Settings.
  • App preferences on your device: language, theme, and bookmarked advertisements — stored locally on your phone, never uploaded.

2. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or files. We show no advertisements and use no advertising trackers.

3. How data is used

  • To display job advertisements and teaching vacancies.
  • To send optional push notifications about new ads and closing deadlines.
  • To operate organization and teacher accounts (login, vacancy review).

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Ireland / EU region) under Google's own privacy terms. Approved teaching vacancies are publicly visible in the app by design.

5. Your rights — access and deletion

You may delete your teaching-portal account at any time from inside the app: open Teaching Jobs → profile → Delete account (trash icon in the dashboard). This permanently removes your account, profile, and posted vacancies. For any other privacy request, contact us at the details below.

6. Children's privacy

The app is intended for job seekers aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version will always be posted at this address.
